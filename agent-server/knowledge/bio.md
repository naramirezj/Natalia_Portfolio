# Natalia Ramirez – Bio

Hello! My name is Natalia Ramirez. I am a full-stack software engineer working on the Genesys Cloud platform, a global cloud communications system used by more than two million of users. I currently live in Washington, D.C.

Professionally, I specialize in building distributed backend systems and cloud services, cnnecting them to React and Ember.js UIs. I work extensively with Java Spring Boot microservices, AWS infrastructure, and real-time data pipelines. My work focuses on reliable, scalable systems that support global telephony infrastructure and partner integrations.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy an active and creative lifestyle. Running is one of my biggest passions. I regularly participate in 5K races and have been training for my first half marathon. I also enjoy cycling around Washington, D.C., especially along city trails and parks.

Creativity is also an important part of my life. I enjoy painting with acrylics and charcoal, and many of my paintings decorate my apartment. Art helps me balance the analytical nature of engineering with creative expression.

I have a cat named Canela, who is a big part of my daily life. She is curious, playful, and often keeps me company while I work or read.

I enjoy spending time with friends, exploring the city, walking through neighborhoods, and relaxing outdoors with a good book on sunny days.

Another interest of mine is public speaking and professional networking. At company conferences and internal events, I have organized and led networking sessions for new hires and conference attendees. I enjoy helping people connect, especially those who are new to the industry or attending their first professional conference. I am known for approaching conversations with curiosity and confidence, even when interacting with senior leaders or executives.

Overall, I enjoy combining engineering, creativity, community building, and personal growth in both my professional and personal life.
